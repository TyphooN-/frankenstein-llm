## What landed

**Benchmark coverage is complete.** `qwen3-embedding-8b`, `qwen3-reranker-8b` and
`qwen25-coder-7b-fim` were benchmarked and published, so `docs/benchmarks/` now

**Placement is now measured, not assumed.** ADR 0006 was decided on capacity
arithmetic and said outright it made no performance claim;
`CANDIDATE-STATUS-2026-09-06.md` listed "live three-GPU placement qualification
(ADR 0006: computed, not exercised)" as open.
[PLACEMENT-MEASUREMENTS.md](reference/PLACEMENT-MEASUREMENTS.md) closes it. Two
effects, very different sizes:

- Carrying layers on the V620: **5–6% prefill, 4–6% decode**. Small.
- Splitting a model that fits on one card: **much larger**, because layer split is
  pipelined and every decoded token crosses every device. gemma4 decodes 23.60
  tok/s split against 40.30 on one card; the embedding weights 29.32 against 65.42.

**Two placement changes followed from that.**

- All four `services/sidecar-*.env` moved off the stale `3,6,2` to `1,0,0`. They
  had kept it after `f2576ac` moved the catalog off it, and the gates start those
  units, so this was live rather than latent. Worth +15.8% and +15.5% prefill to
  embeddings and reranking — the honest column for services that never decode —
  and +38.1% decode to infill.
- `gemma4-heretic` moved from `1,0,1` to `1,0,0` on a measured residency of
  9.88 GiB against the 14.88 GiB upper bound the KV column charges. It was loaded
  through the router at the new placement and answered `PONG`, resident only on
  card0.

## What is fixed but not yet proven

Five of the six failed steps in the 00:38 mission were **two** root causes.

1. **MIOpen under `ProtectHome=read-only`.** MIOpen locks its user database before
   reading it, the database lives under `$HOME`, and the failed lock surfaces as
   `miopenStatusUnknownError` from whatever convolution ran first. That one setting
   failed `wemm-embeddings`, `computer-use-grounding` and
   `generative-media-functional` while looking like three unrelated model bugs.
   `MIOPEN_USER_DB_PATH` and `MIOPEN_CUSTOM_CACHE_DIR` now point into the checkout.
   *Verified only in isolation*: MIOpen was watched creating the exact lock file
   that previously failed, in the redirected directory.
2. **The ASR gate ran in the wrong virtualenv.** `gate_asr.py` and
   `asr_roundtrip.py` import `AutoModelForMultimodalLM`, which the tts venv's
   transformers 4.57 does not have and `venvs/asr`'s 5.16 does. Both died on import
   before touching a GPU. `venvs/asr` was referenced nowhere in the repository
   before this change.

**Neither has been proven by a passing gate.** The boot-time mission run is what
proves them. Expected outcome: `wemm-embeddings`, `computer-use-grounding`,
`generative-media-functional`, `asr` and `tts-asr-roundtrip` pass.

`router-models` is the sixth failure and is **not** addressed here. It fails on
`obliterated` and `phr00ty` for their own reasons; `phr00ty` serves at `temp = 1.5`,
which is a plausible cause of tool-call schema failures and is worth checking

## The `obliterated` context question, answered

You asked for a better explanation. Short version: **I recommend not doing it.**

`obliterated` serves at 131,072 context on `1,1,1`, meaning every token crosses the
throttling V620. It is on all three cards because of its *context*, not its
weights: 20.89 GiB of weights plus 9.14 GiB of KV cache at 131,072 tokens is
30.03 GiB, and the two RX 6900 XTs together offer 25.47 GiB of working headroom.

Halve the context to 65,536 and the KV drops to about 4.57 GiB, total ~25.46 GiB —
which fits the pair, drops the V620, and buys the 5.9% prefill / 6.3% decode
measured above.

Three reasons not to:

1. **The margin is 0.01 GiB.** 25.46 against 25.47 is not a fit you can plan
   against. `gpu_placement.py` says in its own docstring that "fits" is a planning
   statement, not an allocator result. It would need a residency measurement first,
   the way `gemma4-heretic` did.
2. **You pay half your context for about 6%.** 128K to 64K is a real capability
   loss for long documents and long agent sessions.
3. **The comparison that justified the gemma4 change does not hold here.** That one
   was a 70.8% gain, with about 4 GiB of measured headroom, and cost no capability
   at all. This is a tenth of the benefit, on a knife edge, for a real loss.

For completeness, the others are worse: `heretic` needs ≤64,294 and so misses a 64K
context by roughly 1,200 tokens; `fable` would have to halve again to 32,768; and
`phr00ty` would need 6,312 tokens, which is absurd. Those four presets keep `1,1,1`
and the arithmetic is recorded in PLACEMENT-MEASUREMENTS.md so the trade can be
revisited deliberately rather than rediscovered.
